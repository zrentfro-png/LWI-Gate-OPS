Airport Conditions

Airport closure / ramp shutdown

Freezes all local aircraft movement. Taxi and pushback approvals stop. Flights that need to move during the closure are held until reopening, and longer closures can realistically cancel some affected flights.

ATC destination ground stop

Holds only departures going to the selected destination(s). Those flights can taxi normally, but cannot push back until the ATC stop ends. Other destinations continue operating.

Use Ctrl/Cmd-click to select more than one destination.
